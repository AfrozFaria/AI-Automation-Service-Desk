# AI Usage Policy
## Approved tools
Employees may use the company-approved AI assistant for drafting, summarising and translating internal text.
## Prohibited data
Never paste customer personal data, health information or policy numbers into public AI tools. Only the approved internal assistant may process customer data, and only for approved use cases.
## Human review
AI-generated content that goes to customers must be reviewed by an employee before sending.
## Reporting issues
Report incorrect or unsafe AI output to the AI Support Desk using the incident form.
