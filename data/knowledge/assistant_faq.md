# Approved AI Assistant FAQ
## Who can access the assistant?
All employees after completing the 30-minute AI basics module.
## Can it read my emails automatically?
No. It only sees text that you paste or upload into it.
## Why is an answer wrong or made up?
Generative AI can produce confident but incorrect text. Always check facts, numbers and dates against the source system.
## Does it work in Finnish and Swedish?
Yes. Quality is usually best in English and Finnish. Always review translations.
